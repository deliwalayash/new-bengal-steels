About New Bengal Steels

South Gujarat's trusted iron and steel stockist for over 35 years. Authorized distributors of primary steel mills supplying heavy structural steel, parallel flange beams, and high-tensile plates to India's leading PEB manufacturers and EPC contractors.

35+ Years of Trust & Reliability

Pioneering Steel Stockholding & Supply in Surat & Western India

Founded by visionary industrialist Mr. Jatan Adhikari, New Bengal Steels has grown from a regional stockyard into one of Gujarat’s premier stockists and authorized dealers of primary structural steel. Located in the industrial heartland of Sachin GIDC, Surat, we maintain continuous inventory of heavy structural steel, hot-rolled plates, and specialized coils.

Our primary clientele includes Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) manufacturers, heavy fabrication engineering units, infrastructure contractors, bridge builders, and petrochemical projects. Every metric ton of steel that leaves our godown carries complete heat traceability and genuine original Mill Test Certificates (MTC) directly from primary producers.
