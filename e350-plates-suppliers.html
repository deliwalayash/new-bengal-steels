High Yield Strength IS 2062 E350 Plates: The Backbone of Modern PEB Fabrication

Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) manufacturing in India has seen a massive technological transformation. Heavy industrial sheds, logistics warehouses, and clear-span structures now universally specify IS 2062 Grade E350 steel plates rather than standard mild steel (E250). With a guaranteed minimum yield strength of 350 N/mm² (MPa), E350 plates empower structural engineers to design slender, lighter built-up sections that support equal or greater load conditions.

As one of the most reliable E 350 plates suppliers in Surat and South Gujarat, New Bengal Steels maintains comprehensive ready stocks ranging from 6.0 mm to 100.0 mm thickness at our Sachin GIDC stockyard. Sourced directly from integrated primary producers—SAIL, Jindal Steel & Power Ltd (JSPL), and Tata Steel—our plates guarantee superior surface flatness, consistent thickness tolerance, low Carbon Equivalent (CEV) for effortless welding, and sub-zero impact toughness.

How E350 Plates Cut 18% to 22% Weight in PEB Built-Up Sections

In a Pre-Engineered Building portal frame, 70% of the total primary tonnage lies in the tapered rafters and rigid frame columns. Because steel yield capacity jumps from 250 MPa to 350 MPa (a 40% increase in load capacity):

  • Thinner Web Plates: Rafter web plate thicknesses can be scaled down from 8mm to 6mm, drastically lightening the roof load.
  • Reduced Flange Thickness: Built-up flange plates withstand high bending moments with smaller cross-sections.
  • Lower Foundation Loads: Lighter steel frames reduce concrete foundation footings, civil excavation volume, and anchor bolt costs.
  • Freight Economy: Shipping lighter manufactured portal rafters translates to fewer trailer trips and lower transport overheads.
